About AL KHEIR

A practical partner for mobility, equipment & contracting.

We support businesses and project teams with transportation, heavy equipment, skilled manpower and contracting services from our base in Abu Dhabi.

Who We Are

Built around reliable project support.

AL KHEIR INTERNATIONAL GENERAL TRANSPORT AND CONTRACTING LLC provides multiple mobility and contracting solutions to businesses and industries. Our offering covers passenger vehicles, light-duty trucks, medium-duty trucks, heavy-duty trucks, trailers and heavy equipment.

As a general contractor, we also support transportation, infrastructure, electromechanical equipment, water pipeline installation, civil works, drainage and sewerage, earthworks and excavation requirements.

Our approach is straightforward: understand the requirement, mobilize the right resources and deliver professional support with safety and efficiency in mind.
